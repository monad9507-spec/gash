Overview

Hash Broker combines a collectible NFT with a permissionless discovery process. You connect a wallet, start the WebGPU miner and search for a valid SHA-256 proof. A successful proof lets you mint one unique cyber-terminal broker.

02

How minting works

  1. ConnectSwitch your wallet to Robinhood Chain.
  2. MineWebGPU tests nonces against the current challenge.
  3. DiscoverWhen your hash beats the target, the proof is ready.
  4. MintConfirm the transaction and your broker is generated on-chain.
YOUR WALLET IS PART OF THE PROOF.A proof found by another wallet cannot be submitted by you.

03

Difficulty

Difficulty is measured in leading zero bits. For example, 36 bits means the hash must start with enough zero data to satisfy a 36-bit target. Every hash is independent: the closest result shown by the miner is not partial progress toward the next one.

EXPECTED SEARCH TIME2 ^ difficulty / hashrateHigher GPU speed increases your chance per second. The expected time is an average, not a guarantee.

04

On-chain art

The proof and token ID become the seed for the artwork. The SVG, metadata and traits are rendered by the smart-contract renderer and returned as a Base64 data URI. Hash Broker does not use IPFS or an external image server.

05

Staking

Hash Broker staking supports eight reward channels: $MANCER, $PONS, $NVDA, $AMZN, $AAPL, $STONKBROKER, $ZZZ and $CASHCAT.

STAKING ACTIVATES AT HASH BROKER #1,000.Until the collection reaches 1,000 minted NFTs, new staking transactions are blocked by the contract. After activation, approve your NFTs, stake them and claim funded rewards from the STAKE terminal.

06

FAQ

Can I mine from a phone?

The terminal is designed for a desktop browser with WebGPU and a compatible GPU. A phone can connect and monitor the collection, but GPU mining performance may be limited.

Does restarting create progress?

No. Hashes do not accumulate. Restarting only stops the search temporarily; it does not improve or reduce the target.

Is this financial advice?

No. Hash Broker is a collectible experiment. Minting, trading and reward tokens involve risk. Never share your seed phrase or private key.
